Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who we are

Nyumbani Concierge ("we", "us", "our") is a concierge service registered in Nairobi, Kenya. We help Kenyans in the diaspora manage tasks back home — document runs, land and title verification, business registration, bill payments and home-care retainers — with photo/video proof and official receipts.

Our website is nyumbaniconcierge.vercel.app. Our contact email is semacheck254@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

When you use our website and services, we collect the following:

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, and password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Payment information: M-Pesa phone number, M-Pesa confirmation codes, and bank transfer references. We do not store full credit or debit card numbers — all card payments are processed by Safaricom M-Pesa or your bank.
  • Order details: services you request, instructions you provide, and any documents or photos you upload.
  • Communication records: messages you send through the website chat, WhatsApp, or email.
  • Device and usage data: your browser type, device type, IP address, pages visited, and time spent on the site — collected automatically through standard web server logs.

3. How we use your data

We use your data for the following purposes only:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process your orders and deliver the services you purchased.
  • To send you receipts, reports, and job updates via the website and WhatsApp.
  • To communicate with you about your orders, respond to your questions, and resolve disputes.
  • To process M-Pesa and bank transfer payments through our payment partners.
  • To improve our website and services (analysing usage patterns in aggregate).
  • To comply with Kenyan legal and tax obligations (KRA records).

4. How we share your data

We do not sell your personal data to anyone. We share it only in these limited situations:

  • Payment processors: your M-Pesa number and payment details are shared with Safaricom (via Tuma) to process STK push payments. Bank transfer details are shared with no third party — you pay directly into our bank account.
  • Service providers on the ground: when you order a service (e.g. a site visit or document run), we share only the information needed to complete the job (e.g. your name, the property address, or document details) with the person carrying out the task in Kenya.
  • Legal obligation: if required by Kenyan law, court order, or regulatory authority.

5. How we protect your data

  • Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text).
  • All data is stored in encrypted databases (Supabase, which uses AES-256 at rest and TLS in transit).
  • Our website uses HTTPS everywhere — your connection is encrypted.
  • Admin access is protected by a separate passcode not stored in the public codebase.
  • We do not store credit card numbers or full bank account details.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Account data: kept as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal information within 30 days.
  • Order and payment records: kept for 7 years to comply with Kenyan tax law (KRA requirements).
  • Server logs: automatically deleted after 90 days.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (request via email).
  • Correct any inaccurate data (update it in your account settings or email us).
  • Delete your account and personal data (email us — we'll action it within 30 days, subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Export your data in a readable format (we'll provide a copy on request).
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential data processing at any time.

8. Cookies and local storage

We use minimal cookies and browser local storage:

  • Session tokens: stored in local storage to keep you logged in across page loads.
  • Service worker cache: a small cache to make the site load faster on repeat visits. This cache stores static files (HTML, CSS, icons) only — no personal data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

9. Third-party services

The following third-party services process data on our behalf:

  • Vercel — hosts our website (server logs may record your IP address).
  • Render — runs our API server.
  • Supabase — stores account and order data (encrypted PostgreSQL database).
  • Tuma (Safaricom M-Pesa) — processes M-Pesa STK push payments.

10. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you via email or a notice on the website. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version.

12. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, email us at semacheck254@gmail.com or use the WhatsApp button on our website.
